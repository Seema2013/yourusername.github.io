Family plans live in too many places

Messages, paper notes, calendars, school circulars, and memory. The person who holds it all together often spends the first part of every day deciding what to do next.

We think a planning app should take weight off, not add another system to maintain. We’re testing that idea with real families before building more.

What we’re building

We’re starting small: one screen that shows what matters today, and a way to tick things off. The rest comes only after families tell us it’s useful.

Daily plan

Repeating tasks and a view of the week ahead. Planned for later.

Reminders and smart suggestions

Nudges at useful times and plan suggestions that fit your day. Planned for later, and we’re still checking what’s practical.
